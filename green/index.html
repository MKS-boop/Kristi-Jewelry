About Kristi

Where heritage meets the future

KRISTI presents high-value original jewelry by jewelers and silversmiths from around the world to the American and international markets, bringing them together under one branded platform.

Experience shaped across language, art, show business, cinema, design and cultural heritage has helped Kristi develop the ability to communicate with people, understand their preferences and build long-term customer relationships.

Professional experience at Hovnanian Company Inc. in New York, Nina Hovnanian Couture, and Treasures of Armenia — a center for design, handcrafted art, distinctive fashion pieces, jewelry and silverwork — shaped Kristi’s refined approach to customers, product presentation and sales.

In the United States, by combining experience from two worlds, Kristi has built a broad and stable customer network over the years.

Today, the KRISTI name is developing as an umbrella brand, presenting the work of jewelry authors and creators from different countries within one shared branded environment.

KRISTI’s goal is to give these pieces a contemporary online presentation, a marketing environment and a path to new customers.

Rather than simply repeating traditional luxury, the collections presented by KRISTI aim to create a new visual environment in which jewelry can carry memory, possess identity and look toward the future.

Behind Kristi

Born in Yerevan, Kristi is the founder of the KRISTI brand.

In the United States, she initially presented and sold jewelry within a small circle. Over the years, that circle developed into a stable customer network, creating the experience on which KRISTI is growing today.
